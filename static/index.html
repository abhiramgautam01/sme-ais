Detected anomalies

Sales that deviate sharply from the product's local norm, found by a robust median/MAD z-score > 4 and cross-checked by an independent Isolation Forest model. These are excluded from model training so bad data cannot distort forecasts.

How adaptation works

  1. New sales data is merged and cleaned
  2. The model chosen at training time forecasts the new period
  3. Error, bias and staleness are compared to the training baseline
  4. Drifted products re-run the model competition and may switch models
  5. Inventory, staffing and alerts refresh automatically
